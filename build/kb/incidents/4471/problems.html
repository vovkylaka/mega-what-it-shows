parser — the shared parser reads dates month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first. The shared parser reads month first, so day and month swap: 7 April goes out as 4 July. Dates with a day above 12 do not parse as a month, which is why only some bookings move. This is the bug #4471 describes.

hint — booking-v3 tells people to type the month first

alder.physio, copper.studio and pale.harbour run booking-v3. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY under it, so people type the month first, and the form reads the tenant’s way, day first. Same symptom, a different bug: fixing the parser does not fix it. The fix is the hint, or reading the field the way the hint asks.

one-day-move — the date shifts by one day, not by a month

These reports describe a date that moves by one day: Tuesday 12 May shown as Monday 11 May, one meeting that landed on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto, an invite that arrived before the booking was made. A month-first parser cannot turn 12 May into 11 May. This looks like a time-zone conversion, and it needs its own investigation. The desk had already noted these reports (desk-notes.json); the cause table files them under parser by tenant.

How the count was made

  • One id, one report: the export holds some reports twice, so duplicates are removed first.
  • A report’s cause comes from its tenant and the way it came in (causes.ts), not from the intake tag. The intake tag is a guess from the reporter’s browser and disagrees with the tenant often.
  • The reports the desk read as a one-day move are taken out of parser and counted on their own.
